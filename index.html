Romans 3 – 8

No Condemnation

Jews and Gentiles alike stand guilty before God3:9. But God now declares people right through faith in Jesus3:22–24, just as Abraham's faith was credited to him4:3. That brings peace with God5:1, freedom from sin's rule6:11, an answer to the war inside7:24–25, and a love nothing can break8:38–39.
